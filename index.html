Smart garbage collection for Nairobi estates

Smart Sweep watches every bin so you never have to. Sensors inside the bins report how full they are, the system alerts the collection team and plans the shortest route, and residents follow their collection and pay with M-Pesa — no overflowing bins, no wasted trips.

How it works:
1. Bins report themselves — a sensor in each bin measures the fill level and sends it to the system.
2. The system plans the work — dispatchers see the full bins on one dashboard, get alerts, and send drivers on the shortest route.
3. Residents stay in the loop — collection updates, invoices and M-Pesa payments, all in one place.
SMART BINS
Sensors report the fill level of every bin, all day — no bin is checked by guesswork.
LIVE ALERTS
When a bin is nearly full, the collection team is alerted before it overflows.
SMART ROUTES
Drivers only visit the bins that need emptying, on the shortest route — less fuel, less time.
EASY PAYMENTS
Residents get clear invoices and pay for collection with M-Pesa.
